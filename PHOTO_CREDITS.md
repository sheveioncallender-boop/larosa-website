Fonts: **Lora** (regular and italic variable fonts) and **Manrope**, from the [Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts). Their SIL Open Font License files are included alongside the local font files.

Logo: the user-supplied La Rosa transparent PNG from the preceding logo preparation, copied without redesign or recolouring to `larosa_website/static/src/img/la-rosa-logo.png`. La Rosa retains its logo/brand rights. A burgundy-background companion supplied in the same conversation is used for the app icon.
